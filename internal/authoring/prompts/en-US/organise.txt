A step may reach no tool at all: that is the person thinking.
Do not invent a tool outside the list; names outside it are discarded.

The stops_when field is that step's exception, in the person's own words:
the condition under which the run stops there rather than carrying on. Put
it on the step where it happens, not on the agent as a whole — "the customer
cannot be found" belongs to the step that looks the customer up. Leave it
empty only on steps that have no exception.
